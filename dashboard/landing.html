The problem with data is never volume.
It lives in fragments. Systems that were never built
to speak to each other.

Every answer a business needs
is already somewhere in that data.
It just hasn't been read as one thing.

Where it breaks
Financial Services
Risk models running on market data that hasn't touched the credit ledger. Decisions made from one source when three are speaking.
Wholesale / Trade
Demand signal sitting in a rep's workflow. Inventory in an ERP. Chargeback patterns in a spreadsheet. The same account, three systems, no conversation.
Sovereign / Institutional
Macroeconomic feeds, conflict event data, and credit spreads processed in sequence. Never simultaneously. The correlation is always found after the fact.
Supply Chain
Port congestion data, procurement schedules, and customer delivery windows in separate systems. The delay is visible in each one. The impact isn't visible in any.
Healthcare
Clinical outcomes, supply chain, and billing operating in isolation. The operational signal that predicts the clinical problem exists. It's just never been in the same room.
